Motion

  • 150–250 ms, ease-out (quint). Only for state changes and card swaps.
  • Card swap: content cross-fades in place, height animates. Expand and shrink follow the finger.
  • No decorative motion. The particle sun is the only ambient movement.

Copy

  • Buttons are verbs: "Suggest times", "Send", "Check again".
  • No filler states: not "Answer ready", "Your turn", "Success".
  • Header = source + person or place. Hero = the one thing to read.
  • A card that commits always shows the exact content it will send.
