Scrolling sideways

When there are several options, swipe sideways. Two kinds. A carousel when each option needs its own space (photo, why, actions); the next card peeks in so you know there's more. A strip inside one card for small things (photos, time slots). Choosing isn't sending, so choices use the orange outline, never solid orange.

Talk to any card

No dock. Hold the side Essential Key to talk; pull down anywhere to type. Either way you're talking to the card in front, shown by its name tag. What you say becomes a draft; only a tap sends. Buttons are suggestions, never the only way.
