## 1. What it is

An agent-first Android home screen. You talk; Misura works across apps in the background; the screen shows what it's doing and asks only when it needs you. Built on the **Nothing Phone (3a)** (demo + prototype). The **square concept phone** on the landing page is the vision piece; both use one system (§3).

A fun, portfolio and content project, not a startup bet: no demand tests here.

**Goal (restated 12 Nov):** control the whole phone by voice, across apps, well enough to use daily and to make a demo that wows. Few hard-coded routes: Misura gets general abilities, not one tool per feature. Banking, IDs, logins and paying are out of scope.

**Any Android phone, anyone (build principle, 18 Nov):** it is open source, so nothing is tied to one phone or one person. Device-specific values are read or learned at runtime (screen size, density, the talk key, the speech language, the phone's name), never hard-coded; a feature a phone lacks falls back (no side key = Hold to talk button). Personal data (briefing, keys, contacts) lives only in local, git-ignored files or on the phone, never in code or defaults. The Nothing Phone (3a) is the test phone, not a target; a value that only holds there is named as an example in a comment, not built in.

## 2. Interaction model

Two layers:

| Layer | What | Tappable |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen** | Your words (said, then echoed), Misura's steps as they happen, outcomes, home (clock, widgets, coming up, recent tasks) | To open or show more (task row → task, step → history, event → event); never to commit |
| **Cards** | Only when you have something to do: pick, decide, answer, approve | Yes; everything you act on |

Rules:
1. **Screen talks, cards ask.** State is never a card.
2. **✓ means done, nothing else.** The orange dot tells whose turn it is: a **still dot** is Misura talking (its reason on a place card); a dot with two small ones **circling** it is Misura working, one speed everywhere, alone under your words between steps so the screen is never blank; an **empty ring that beats** means ready for your go (no word; it replaced *will* on 14 Nov). ✕ = failed, always with a card that offers the fix.
3. **One tap commits.** Solid orange sends, books or creates. Everything the tap does is on screen when you tap: the card says it. **Several messages, one go (built 15 Nov):** messages to several people sit on one card, people with the same words in one block; one tap on "Send 3" sends them all, each as its own tracked task with its own ✓/✕ line. **A spoken "go" also counts** (built 15 Nov, untested with a real voice), in this form only: through Hold to talk, the whole utterance is go / send / send it / send them / yes, matched by the app's code (the model never decides you meant go), for the exact card that was in front when the button went down and still is; then 3 seconds of Undo before anything leaves (built 16 Nov: it also covers the card of a message typed in an app, the one that names the chat and the exact words; a stopped pay, book or delete tap still needs your tap; Undo is checked again after the wait, so a late Undo always wins). Typed words never count. The word **Approve** is kept for a tap that pays, books or deletes inside an app (the commit gate's card): tap only. Everything else you say prepares and edits.
4. **Back walks the cards.** Nav line on top: ‹ back one card on the left; where you are and "n steps ›" on the right (opens the history). System back gesture = ‹. At the root of a task, back goes home. The system home gesture always goes home (Misura is the launcher). A finished task (message sent) folds home when you leave the screen, never on a timer; words on a finished task start a new one.
5. **Talk to the card in front.** While you speak, the card you're on docks small at the bottom; what you say is about it.
6. **Context travels.** A task's steps fold into its nav line.
6a. **Home holds only what is open** (14 Nov). Lines for what you started: the beating ring when it is ready for your go (a tap opens the task), ✕ when it failed, the working sign for what runs (by name when it is one task, "Working on 3 things" when several). Open tasks older than a day leave home; a failed one leaves after an hour, unless it was a message that may not have gone out (that keeps the day). Swipe a line left past halfway to dismiss it into Earlier (also a TalkBack Dismiss action); running agent work stops first, a waiting draft stays intact and nothing is sent. A grouped working line dismisses every task it represents. Earlier uses the same swipe with Delete: permanently remove the conversation, its turns and owned item photos, with no undo, discarding waiting drafts through the controller first. New words on a reopened task follow the existing old-task rules. Finished tasks are one tap away behind "Earlier ›". **Cards on home are only for what comes to you or runs on a clock** (someone wrote, a ride, the next turn); a text you dictated is a line, not a card.
7. **A task is a thread.** What you say next on a task takes a clean screen: your words on top, its steps, the answer. Everything said before sits above it, one scroll up, quieter (your words, what Misura did, what it said). "n steps ›" shows the same thread from the start. Still screen text: nothing in it commits.

Reference flow (Run 1, both form factors): say "best bibimbap in Kreuzberg" → steps → map on top + place carousel at the bottom (pin follows the card, Misura's pick marked) → tap → place detail (‹ 3 places) → talk: "Friday 19:30 for four with FriendC, FriendB, ColleagueA, let them know" → steps → *will* steps + question card (exact message, **Book & send**) → "Booked." + ✓ steps → home. Then: "build me a step counter" → steps → preview card (**Add to home**) → widget on home.

- **Card recipe (14 Nov):** text starts where the button's label starts (one left edge); a quiet label, then one focal thing (the exact words or one big number, never both), then buttons; label 4 above its content, 14 above the buttons; no explaining sentence, the button says what the tap does ("Send 3", "Approve €20").
- **Cards:** Place card (carousel), Map (strip above carousel; full screen on tap), Question card (approve, offers, incoming, problems), Docked card, Live (music; the one card that's a control, not a question).
- **Buttons:** solid orange commits · orange outline moves forward · grey outline everything else.
- **Icons:** steps show the real app's own one-colour mark (themed-icon layer from the installed app, tinted text-2); line icon when the app has none or the step isn't one app. No coloured app badges.
- **Task swipe (variant C):** line follows the finger 1:1; a surface band (8 u corners, 3 u vertical / 6 u right inset, running out to the screen edge) reveals an 11 u drawn cross and semibold task-size Dismiss/Delete in text-2, fading in by 40 u and switching to text past half width. Release past halfway: 200 ms out, then 180 ms height collapse; shorter: 180 ms return. No movement with animations disabled; normal timings follow system animator scale. Vertical scrolling, tap-to-open and mandatory system gesture edges stay available.
- **Home layout:** "now" group on top (clock left, compact widgets right); at the bottom the open tasks as lines and "Earlier ›"; cards only for what comes to you or runs on a clock (rule 6a).

## 4. Architecture

Detail: `superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-misura-demo1-design.md` (§5 routes, §6 controller), `superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-misura-brain-design.md` (agent loop).

- **Phone-only app** (Kotlin, launcher). Modules: `:core` (pure JVM logic), `:app` (Android), `:testapp` (fake chat). State local (Room). Dismissal is a backward-compatible `dismissedAt` field in the existing session JSON, using the home age filter; no schema change. Explicitly dismissed rows stay beyond Earlier’s 30 completed-row cutoff, within its existing 200-session lookback. Stop and removal are serialized on the agent’s session queue. New words or a fresh go on a reopened draft clear dismissal; opening alone does not. Room-backed reads and turn appends keep Keystore and JSON work on Dispatchers.IO, including when called from UI polling; Room dispatching SQL alone does not cover decryption. Photo cleanup is limited to paths saved in the session’s turns; captures interrupted before a result have no session ownership and keep the existing seven-day cache ceiling. Deletion keeps controller send evidence: a send already approved cannot be recalled; dismissed lists do not automatically continue after that send.
- **Local setup (19 Nov):** an unconfigured first launch opens non-exported `setup/SetupActivity`; a configured phone skips it. Reopen through **Misura settings** in the unfiltered swipe-down app sheet; no home-screen control. Choose Vertex credentials or a Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic key. Google Cloud and external search credentials are optional for API-key routes. Validation is offline (JSON/type/email/RSA key and safe project/header input), not proof of Cloud permissions/billing. Save writes atomically through the existing cipher, preserves blank secret fields, restores previous values on failed persistence and clears successfully saved input; saved keys never prefill. Password fields keep unsaved text out of saved UI state; the activity blocks screenshots/recents. Setup never calls the agent or model. A setup-only `setup_project` override takes precedence; without it, legacy key-project/adb-token-project rules remain. Native buttons open notification/accessibility/home settings, request voice/alerts and optional read-only contacts/calendar permissions, and request Shizuku authorization or open its app. Shizuku still requires manual install/pair/start, and Android restricted settings/battery policy may need manual adjustment. Notification switches list current posting and saved blocked packages so a dismissed source can still be unblocked. All packages default on; blocking does not delete history.
- **Model routing (20 Nov):** `ModelRoutes` captures one immutable configuration inside the turn timeout, retaining main/hand clients and account-bound Google token cache through nested tools and isolated memory checks. Native-only startup makes no Google sign-in request. Photo requests capture their own coherent current Google account/project and isolate caches on rotation. Direct Places/search tools use captured Google/search inputs. Both Tool.run overloads must bind that route: Kotlin delegation otherwise sends the progress-callback overload to the credential-free metadata prototype (repaired 21 Nov). Missing Google removes find_places/show_places/directions from specs and dispatch; prompt routes places to installed Maps. Place discovery/recommendation uses find_places then show_places, ahead of general web lookup. A renewed place search uses current tool availability rather than treating a prior turn’s auth/search failure as permanent; web-named places must first resolve through find_places before native cards. Web questions use look_up first and may use an installed browser only after needs_browser, within existing budgets. No credential snapshot enters session history.
- **Three parts kept separate:** brain (agent loop), renderer (screen + cards), hands (routes). A **Controller** owns every task and side effect: saved task states, prepare/commit/check/cancel with evidence, approval bound to the exact rendered content, never a blind retry, late answers never overwrite.
- **Network destinations:** Vertex uses `aiplatform.googleapis.com`, Gemini uses `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`, OpenAI uses `api.openai.com` and Anthropic uses `api.anthropic.com`. Optional search sends the lookup to `api.exa.ai`, `api.tavily.com` or `api.context.dev`. Native lookup uses the captured model account and can incur search charges. Access depends on provider, model, quotas and account settings. Google results requiring suggestion HTML yield browser fallback.
- **Conversation continuity:** visible history is portable across providers. Tool calls and their results stay paired, while opaque reasoning/signatures can be reused only by the matching client. OpenAI uses stateless requests. After interruption, completed results survive and an already-started action may have an unknown outcome. Check the target before repeating it.
- **Agent loop** (`core/agent/Agent.kt`): per-session ordered queue, ≤ 8 tool/authorization calls, 60 s per model call for the main model, the HTTP read wait included (30 s until 16 Nov: the call after a long app run carries its items and timed out; the HTTP wait stayed 30 s until the night of 16 Nov, so the 60 s was not real), 300 s per turn on the phone (90 s default in code): the sum of a first call 60 s, a hand run 150 s + 30 s grace and the final call 60 s, a fixed sum, not the time that is left; the selected Vertex/Gemini/OpenAI/Anthropic model, captured once per turn with credentials and both role settings (Vertex retains its service-account or adb-token route); tools: `find_places` (Maps grounding; **where:** a place the model passes as `near` wins; no `near` (or "here"/"me") uses where the phone is now, read per search by `brain/PhoneLocation.kt` (approximate permission enough; a fresh cached fix from any provider, else one 5 s request; no fix older than 10 min; nothing stored; Android answers only while a Misura screen is in front, so a routine or a run started from another app gets "unknown") and sent to Google only, as the grounding `latLng` and a 20 km Places text-search bias; the model sees only `searchedNear` = the named place, "where the phone is now" or "unknown", never the phone's coordinates (returned places keep their own coordinates); "near me"-style `near` values count as no place; unknown → the prompt says search near home if known and say so, else ask; names grounding didn't match get their id from a Places text search only when the name is identical; then Places details: hours for the week, rating + count, price, kind, up to 3 photos with author credits), `directions` (a placeId from this session’s find_places results only; opens Maps’ public directions URL with navigate requested and mode chosen by Maps; the step names the place, then “Opened.”), `show_places` (place ids from find_places + pick + one line), `ask` (question + ≤ 4 choices), `answer` (plain text: one short sentence for a fact or outcome, up to about 120 words for an explanation or a view; no markdown), `look_up` (the selected external search service first, then the captured model provider’s native search. A terminal empty result spends no further search budget. A direct lookup has a shared 55-second limit. Missing or unusable sources return `needs_browser`. Complete results carry a session-scoped lookup ID, exact answer and verified source links. Google results requiring search-suggestion HTML fall back because the native screen has no compliant renderer), `remember` (built 14 Nov, `app/.../brain/Remember.kt`: Misura's own notes, see below), `draft_message` (who + exact text; code resolves the chat in any chat app with a reply button (MessagingStyle, one-to-one, live notification with an app-owned id), exact name or a unique first name; the same full name across apps picks the newest message, and creates a task waiting for approval; an unknown name goes back to the model with the chats it can use). The model only picks ids and writes short lines; every place fact on screen comes from tool data. Each tool call is a **step** (label + result from the tool's code, ●/✓/✕, saved with the session). Follow-ups carry the place you're looking at as a note. Words said while a turn runs cancel that turn (it runs as its own job on the session's queue) and go next. **Lists (built 13 Nov):** several things asked at once are worked through in order; a draft with `more` set keeps the task open, and when its send is confirmed (`Controller.onSent` → `Agent.taskSent`, wherever you are; the open task's screen checks too, for a confirm the process died after) the model gets a turn to carry on. That turn is not speech: no echo, same turn number, so the list's ✓ lines stay together. How to pick places ("best" = most recommended at any price) is in the system prompt. **Who you are (built 14 Nov):** the last part of the prompt is a briefing, your own text file put on the phone with `scripts/push-about.sh` (default `about.local.md`, kept out of git; first 6000 characters; read fresh on every model call). Without one, a neutral built-in line applies (18 Nov; it held his own taste before). After it come **Misura's own notes** (`remember`): short dated lines it keeps when you tell it something lasting or ask it to remember; `replaces` corrects or forgets one. On the phone only (encrypted preferences), retained until changed or explicitly forgotten; only the newest notes that fit the prompt budget are included. Every write is a step on screen ("Noted: …" ✓, "Forgot: …"), so nothing is remembered unseen. **Memory takes the user's word only:** once any other tool has read outside text in a turn (a web page, an app's screen, place data), `remember` is refused in code for the rest of that turn. `replaces` must match exactly one note. Notes have their own 2500 characters of the prompt, newest first in line, and are handed to the model as facts, never instructions. Each model call also starts with today's date. **Thread:** `thread(turns)` (`core/agent/Thread.kt`) reads your words and Misura's own lines back from the saved turns, including the code-accepted cited answer and clickable source links; a tap on a choice carries its exchange on.
- **Calendar, `calendar` (built 19 Nov):** reads expanded events (up to 30) from visible Android calendars and finds free time (busy timed instances only, default 08:00–20:00); creates use a code-owned Add/Don’t card, one insert of the exact event per yes within five minutes in the same session, revoked on stop, with READ_CALENDAR/WRITE_CALENDAR granted over adb and no API key or app UI.
- **Contacts, `contacts` (built 19 Nov):** read-only Android contacts lookup by name returns up to five people, exact display-name matches first, then starts-with and contains, with phone type labels and emails; READ_CONTACTS is granted over adb, no runtime prompt, and the step uses the phone’s contacts app mark.
- **The general hand, `use_app(app, goal)`** (built 12 Nov, `app/.../hands/UseApp.kt`): opens any installed app on the hidden screen and works it until the goal is met. With `near_user` (24 Nov) it is told the phone's city, gets an `area` tool, and its results carry `searchArea` and `userIn` (decisions log 24 Nov evening). Each round it reads the screen as numbered lines (everything with words, or that can be tapped or typed into), a separate selected-provider model call picks one action (tap, type, key back/enter, scroll, wait, done, fail), code performs it. No per-app code. Limits: 25 actions, 150 s (kept inside the loop: a run that runs out of time returns what it recorded, error first line "took too long"); the turn allows 300 s. The step line shows each action live ("Clock: tap “Add timer”"), then settles on "Clock: <goal>" ✓; Misura's answer is the outcome ("Timer set."). The model is told the installed apps (name = package). **Commit gate (built 13 Nov, `CommitGate.kt`):** a tap on a button whose words (visible text or hidden label, English and German) say send, pay, buy, order, book, delete, post, share, call… is refused in code. The run stops, its step reads *will*, and Misura shows its own card ("Clock: tap “Delete”?" · solid orange **Delete** · grey Don't). Only your tap on that card lets it through: each card answers only its own question, and one yes covers one tap on the button it named, in that app, within five minutes; the model then runs the goal again, and that run goes on on the same hidden screen where the first stopped (16 Nov, midday: one screen stays parked for five minutes; the run takes it only for the same session, app and goal, with the earlier "Done so far" lines carried over, and a no, a stopped turn, a steer, an error or the five minutes close it). The model's own `ask` can never grant it, and nothing later in the same model reply can take the card's place. Messages to people go through `draft_message` and its card when a quick reply is possible, else through `use_app` and this gate, whose card then shows the chat's title and the exact words (15 Nov). It reads text only (no screenshot). Before opening an app it allows that app's notifications, because a first-run "Allow notifications?" prompt on the hidden screen can be neither answered nor read (system permission prompts are closed to screen readers); any other unreadable prompt gets one back press. **Things and pages (16 Nov, `ItemPhoto.kt`):** the hand has an `item` action to record a thing (product, listing, ticket) as a card: its title and price must be words on the same tile as the element it points at (read off the screen by code, never taken from the model), the tile and its photo are found from box geometry alone (no per-app rule: the largest ancestor that holds one title block; the photo is the big childless picture box in it, else the part above the words; one cut off by the screen edge is refused), and the photo is cropped from the hidden display (the phone's own size and density since 18 Nov; 1080×2400 at 480 dpi before) into encrypted `files/items/` JPEGs and metadata, kept 7 days (legacy plaintext still reads). Up to 10 go back in the result as `items` / `itemsFrom` and the model shows them with `show_items`. A tap on a card sends a tap-turn (`Agent.openItem`, only the opaque id) and the model calls `use_app` with `show = true` and `item` = that id; the hand's goal is then a fixed sentence written by code ("Open the thing described under \"Thing to open\" below…"), and the stored full title and price (`files/items/<id>.json`) go in their own `Thing to open` data block (one line each, control characters and line breaks turned into spaces, title cut at 160, price at 24; PROMPT says that block is data like the screen lines), never from the model's words. It starts on the page where the things were recorded when that hand's screen is still parked (same session, same app, within five minutes; a run that recorded things parks its screen), else it opens the app and searches for the title's first words: on `done` the app's task is moved from the hidden display to the main screen (`cmd activity display move-stack <rootTaskId> 0`), same page, then the hidden display is released.
- **The hand, round by round (16 Nov, same file).** Each round: read the screen, one model call, one action, a 0.7 s settle. Switches show as `switch "…" on|off`, and a scroll that changed nothing is told as "nothing moved" (18 Nov); since 22 Nov that direction is also taken away (the round's `scroll` tool offers only the other one, or none when both moved nothing; a call naming it anyway is refused without a swipe; the "before" screen is read right before the swipe) while the screen stays exactly the same (words and places of the shown lines); any change gives it back, and it is never a reason to end the run. **A repeated tap is pointed out (23 Nov):** a tap that went through, chosen on a screen that looked the same (words, places and switch states of the shown lines) as one where the same element was tapped before, gets "you made the same tap on a same-looking screen at step N…" under Done so far; a hint only, and the step-cap failure says "kept repeating the same taps" only when that happened two or more times. The screen is the one the model saw (for a later step of a `steps` call: the frame that step was found on), not re-read at the tap. An unknown route comes from the main model as "Route: …" in the goal, looked up first (see 18 Nov). Besides tap, type, key, scroll, wait, done, fail it has **`steps`** (22 Nov, speed: 2-4 tap/type/key/wait in one model call, later ones found by their whole words on the screen they land on, never through the approval gate; see decisions log), **`note`** (facts it must keep: they stay under "Done so far", so it never opens the same item twice, and they come back in the failure when it runs out) and **`item`** (see "Things and pages" above; several `item` calls in one reply are all recorded against one fresh read of the screen, and the prompt says it early and plainly: call `item` for every thing whose title it can read, partly off screen included, all in the same reply, then scroll; live, one item per round cost a 5-15 s model call each). **A cut-off photo is scrolled into view by the code, not by a model round (16 Nov, morning):** an `item` refused only because its photo is cut off (top bar, screen bottom, the list's own edge) makes the hand work out how far the tile must move so tile and photo sit inside the list's visible band (48 px margin; photo alone if both cannot fit), swipe that far inside the list (never a tap; a move under 150 px is stretched so it can't read as one), settle 0.7 s, read again, find the thing by its exact title on exactly one tile, and record it through the usual checks. At most two swipes per item (the first is a guess: boxes are cut at the screen edge, so a photo running off it shows only its visible part); none or several tiles with that title, new words from the user, or the budget gone leave the old "scroll it into view" message for the model. Items fully visible in a reply are recorded first; the cut-off ones then go top to bottom, each against a fresh read, and not at all once the asked count is reached. The log line `scroll repair attempts=n px=n ms=n` carries numbers only. **A correction said while it works** reaches the running hand without a restart (`Tool.steer`): it keeps the open screen, sees the words under "The user added", the step reads "got it, adjusting", and the words void any yes not yet used (a "don't" must never ride on an old yes). Only a hand that works alone takes words; with two tasks in apps at once the words stop the step and start over, as before. **The yes also dies when a run ends in any error or give-up** (network, took too long, step limit, outer timeout, fail), not only when the turn is stopped; the exit that asks you keeps it for the follow-up run. **Time:** the 150 s budget is checked in the loop and again when the model's reply comes back: a late reply records what it names with `item` (that only reads) and does nothing else. **A commit tap rechecks the budget** after it re-reads the screen and just before the yes is used up: past the budget it gives up ("took too long") and the yes is revoked. **Stops at the asked number:** `use_app` takes an optional `count` (how many things to record, up to 10; ignored with `show`); the hand is told "Record N things, then you are done", and the run ends by itself as a normal success ("recorded N things", items and where they came from) the moment N are recorded, with no further model call, unless the user has just said something new. Live the hand had kept recording to the step limit (about 140 s for the charger ask). **Record first:** while nothing is recorded, at rounds 6 and 12 the model is told once to record what is on screen before any more filtering or sorting; the prompt says that conditions and order in the goal ("cheapest", "at least 60 W") are judged by the main model from the recorded titles and prices, which is also told to pass the plain search goal and, as `count`, the number the user asked for (up to 5 when conditions mean it must pick). **Small repairs:** `back` that closes the app opens it again once per run; a network blip on the hand's call is retried once after 1.5 s (sign-in and 4xx errors are not retried); a picture is accepted for an element only if it shares a band with its words (above/below: same width range, beside: same height range) and no other text block sits between them, so a neighbour's photo is not borrowed; the blocks stacked in the element's own band count as the same thing (beside: its column; above or below: on its side of the picture), so pointing at the price, not the title, of a picture-above tile still finds the photo. The log has one line per round with milliseconds for screen, model and action plus node and line counts, never any text. `ScreenReader` refreshes only the root and the fields, not every node: a 1,791-node page went from 5.5 s to 1.65 s.
- **Opening apps yourself** (app sheet built 18 Nov evening): swipe down on plain home for the reverse app list and local search; the home field otherwise talks to Misura. Pinned apps stay nearest the field, shortcuts can live beside them, and launch counts order All apps. - A draft that waits is only a line with a ring on home. If drafts get overlooked, promote ones older than a few minutes to a card.
- Spoken "go" has only been exercised with a fake voice in tests (the emulator's microphone hears nothing): does the phone's recogniser return a bare "go" reliably, and does it ever hear "go" for "no"? Check on the phone before relying on it.
